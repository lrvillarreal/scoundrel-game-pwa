    THE RULEBOOK
    
      
    How to survive
    
      
    Your dungeon is 44 cards: 26 monsters (♣/♠ 2–A), 9 weapons (♦ 2–10), and 9 potions (♥ 2–10). You start at 20 HP.
    
      
    01
    Explore a room
    Four cards appear. Choose any three in any order; the untouched card carries into the next room. In the final short room, resolve every card.
    
      
    02
    Or avoid it
    Before choosing a card, send the entire four-card room to the bottom of the deck in the same order. You can't avoid two rooms in a row.
    
      
    03
    Fight a monster
    Bare hands cost the monster's full value in HP. A legal weapon reduces damage to max(0, monster − weapon). You may always fight bare-handed instead.
    
      
    Weapon example
    ♦8 beats ♠7 for 0 damage, so 7 becomes its last defeated value. You can then use ♦8 against ♣5, but not ♠9. A ♦8 versus ♠10 would deal 2 damage and would not update the last defeated value.
    
      
    04
    Find gear & medicine
    Equipping a diamond discards your old weapon and its stacked monsters, and resets the limit. A heart heals up to 20 HP. Only one potion works per room, including when already at full health.
    
      
    05
    Win or lose
    Resolve all cards to win and score your remaining HP. Reach 0 HP and lose: your score is negative the total value of monsters still unresolved in the room and deck.
    
      
    Keyboard: Tab and Enter/Space to choose cards; arrow keys move between cards; Escape closes dialogs. The game saves automatically in this browser.
